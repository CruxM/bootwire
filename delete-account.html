Delete your Bootwire account and data

Bootwire for Android, by Wire Studio

Signing in to Bootwire is optional. If you signed in with Google, your Bootwire account exists only on your phone: your name and Google account ID. We don't keep a copy on any server (Bootwire has none), so deleting it from the phone deletes it completely.

In the app

  • Open Bootwire and tap the info button (i) at the top right of Home to open About.
  • Tap Account (it shows "Hi" and your first name when you're signed in).
  • Tap Delete account and data, then Delete. Your name and Google account ID are deleted from the phone straight away. You can keep using Bootwire as a guest.

Sign out on the same screen does the same for your account data.

Delete everything

To also remove Bootwire's activity history, downloaded images and settings, go to Android Settings, Apps, Bootwire, Storage, and tap Clear storage, or uninstall Bootwire.

What gets deleted

  • Your name and Google account ID: deleted when you tap Delete account and data or Sign out.
  • Activity history, downloaded images, settings and the saved Pro status: deleted when you clear Bootwire's storage or uninstall it.

What is kept

  • Nothing is kept by us, because we never receive it.
  • Bootwire Pro belongs to your Google Play account, not to the sign-in, so deleting your Bootwire account doesn't cancel a Pro subscription or remove a purchase. To stop paying, cancel the subscription in Google Play (Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions). Google keeps the purchase record under its own privacy policy.
  • Drive backups you saved (Pro) stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.
  • Your Google account itself isn't affected. To remove Bootwire's sign-in connection, visit https://myaccount.google.com/connections.
