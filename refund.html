Refund policy

Bootwire is free to use. Only Bootwire Pro is paid: a subscription through Google Play, billed monthly, every 3 months or yearly.

Cancelling

You can cancel anytime in Google Play: open Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, choose Bootwire Pro and tap Cancel subscription, or tap Manage subscription on Bootwire's Pro screen. The subscription then won't renew, and you keep Pro until the end of the period you've already paid for. Cancelling doesn't refund the current period by itself; see below.

In the first 48 hours

Google Play handles refund requests made within 48 hours of a payment. Open Google Play, go to Payments and subscriptions, Budget and order history, choose Bootwire Pro and ask for a refund, or use Google Play's refund page.

After 48 hours

Email rmayank165@gmail.com within 30 days of a payment, with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.). We refund that payment if:

  • a Pro feature doesn't work as described on your phone, and we can't fix it; or
  • a drive Bootwire made won't start your PC, and we can't solve it together.

Refunds go back through Google Play to the way you paid. A refunded subscription ends straight away, and Pro is removed from that Google account.

Bought Pro before subscriptions?

If you bought Pro as a one-time purchase before it became a subscription, it stays unlocked for good, with nothing to renew or cancel.

Your rights

This policy doesn't take away any rights you have under consumer law where you live.
